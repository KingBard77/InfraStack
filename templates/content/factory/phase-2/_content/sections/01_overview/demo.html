Overview

__TOOL_TITLE__ is an InfraStack prompt-driven workspace for turning a short __DOMAIN__ brief into a first-pass visual model. The workspace reads explicit terms such as scope, zones, placement layers, ingress, workload tier, data tier, observability controls, and external links, then uses that normalized model to render the stage, technical inventory, prompt notes, and export state.

Use the workspace first. Start with a prompt or preset, generate the initial model, then refine it through the inspector and stage instead of rebuilding the model from scratch.

This workspace is most useful when you need to sketch a baseline quickly, explain placement to other teams, compare design tradeoffs, prepare review diagrams, or save an editable version of a design and reopen it later.
